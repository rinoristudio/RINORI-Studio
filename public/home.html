✿ Our Story ✿

Made slowly, made with love

Rinori Studio is a tiny handmade studio. Every piece is cut, gathered and stitched by hand, one at a time, inspired by soft Japanese colours: sakura pinks, evening blues and quiet paper whites.

Because each one is made by hand, no two are exactly alike. That little difference is what makes it yours.
